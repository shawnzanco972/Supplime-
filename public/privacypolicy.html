Supplime and your health data

Supplime is a personal app. It reads sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and steps from Health Connect only to show how they change while you take your supplements.

All data stays on this phone. Supplime has no server and sends nothing anywhere. Data only leaves the phone if you export a backup or choose to share a summary with an AI assistant yourself.

You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect settings.
